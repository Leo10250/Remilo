# Review the actual intended use

Measure encoded format, dimensions, alpha, orientation and color profile before transformations. Preserve original source bytes. Distinguish tagged sRGB, actual conversion and an explicitly stated interpretation of untagged pixels. Never claim that resizing or interpolation adds source detail.

Inspect original and decoded staged exports. Check recognition, requested details, edge/alpha halos, unwanted text or interface remnants, gradient quality, relevant backgrounds and actual small sizes. Compare paired variants when geography or identity should remain consistent. Lossless encoding preserves transformed pixels; it does not make a resized derivative identical to the original.

Use aspect-preserving cover/contain previews for representative placements. Account for overlays and changed coordinate systems after trimming. A defining cue may be unable to fit with another cue in a shallow crop; explain that conflict rather than silently omitting it. Geometry and histograms do not establish semantic recognition or visual quality.

The helper provides basic decoded-file panels. Make additional crop, dark/light, checkerboard, mask or native-vector previews when they resolve an actual review question; do not require every preview mode for every asset. Keep these previews local. Record brief actual findings for publication; pending or failed findings must not become passes automatically.

Inspect consequential multi-variant work from both the file/export and visual/use perspectives. Independent review is useful when available, not a compulsory two-agent ritual. Human artwork acceptance and runtime acceptance remain separate from technical checks. Synthetic previews cannot establish actual text contrast, accessibility, Android packaging, OEM masks or alarm behavior.
