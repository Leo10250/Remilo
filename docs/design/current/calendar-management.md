# Calendar management — owner-approved 12 October 2026

The owner approved independent deletion, a separate management screen, and
cancellation notices in both Agenda and Details. This correction governs P4-B
presentation and supersedes the earlier placement of full Calendar status before
the reminder title. Task status belongs only in the backlog.

## Hierarchy and navigation

Details order is Title, primary alert time, local operational state/permissions,
conditional concise Calendar exception, Event/Due, Repeat, List/Notes, Schedule
details, then a compact Google Calendar navigation row. Existing persistent local
actions retain their behavior. The row uses shared neutral icon/chevron geometry;
normal status distinguishes Linked · Manual updates and Linked · Automatic sync.
Successful sync, remote observation and failed attempts remain separate facts.

The separate compact Calendar management page shows the reminder context, original
account/calendar, concise sync status and current problems. Optional observation
and attempt timestamps belong in collapsed Sync details. Opening performs local reads only.
Foreground synchronization is independent of navigation. Check is explicit for
manual/read-only context and may be abandoned; Back ignores late UI
results. Review performs its own fresh native preview. Terminal records permit
checks without editing or reopening. Unlink is a visible neutral action here and
remains a shortcut in Reminder actions. Unknown mutations retain captured recovery.

[P4-D](../../calendar-sync.md#status-buttons-and-review-notices) distinguishes
Not linked, manual links, automatic links, pending work, true conflicts, paused
relationships and unknown sent writes. Review changes is prominent when needed;
Sync now and Check update result have phase-specific meanings. Neutral outlined
Unlink with an unlink symbol occupies its own lower group. Open in Google Calendar
is a wrapping external-link text action near source information, with at least
48 dp height. Review confirmations retain 56 dp targets. Management has no
Previous Calendar operations section; confirmed user history appears in Activity.

Agenda retains its filters while an unfiltered count opens a secondary Calendar
review queue. A repeating family counts once, with affected occurrence scopes
and current local alert consequences. Quiet grouped review notifications open the
current queue and never apply a resolution. Dismissal does not clear saved issues.

The [P4-E managed-calendar extension](../../calendar-managed-sync.md) adds explicit
setup and policy review before automatic calendar creation/import/publication.
Settings shows the bound calendar and actual setup phase, selected import/export
behavior and initial recovery alert consequences. Existing calendar candidates,
unknown creation results and access/ownership problems receive specific recovery
actions. The review queue distinguishes incoming events that have not yet become
reminders from existing reminder problems; a recurring family counts once.
Captured work and confirmed outcomes remain separate. Local only preserves both
copies and delivery while stopping applicable transfers/re-adoption. This adds no
full administrative block before Details' primary local reminder information.

## Calendar setup and feedback — 14 October 2026

The owner approved a compact guided page and the dedicated Remilo calendar.
The Google Calendar overview leads with one status card containing the problem,
consequence and next action. Account/source identity follows, then preferences,
review/linked reminders and Advanced manual tools. An owned manual publication
destination remains a separate permission and is not a setup prerequisite.

Setup shows the account and a source tile for the found or proposed Remilo
calendar. Multiple matches open a single-choice picker; incomplete discovery
cannot justify creation. An unavailable saved source requires access recovery or
a separate explicit replacement review. Time zone belongs beside proposed
creation. Settings navigation reads saved state; explicit setup/check actions
may prepare fresh native previews.

Two directional choices say Add Google events to Remilo and Add new Remilo
reminders to Google. An Alert type disclosure and surfaced All-day alert time
row share the imported-alert group. The clock row uses the chosen display format,
a clock icon, chevron, at least 48 dp and a calendar-zone subtitle. Fresh quiet
recovery is explained separately from subsequent Alarm defaults. Google reminder
suppression remains separate explicit consent, initially off. Essential alert and
creation consequences remain visible; detailed recurrence/local-action rules use
contextual disclosures.

Changes coalesce into a new native preview. Checking settings is explicit and
prevents submitting an older preview; obsolete responses cannot replace newer
choices. Turn on sync actually captures the reviewed operation on that press.
After acknowledgement, replace the form with saved progress/results rather than
a disabled-color form. Calendar verification, initial scan completion, imports,
local scheduling and remaining remote work are separate facts. Show counts where
known, never invented percentages or a sample as a full total. A native Ready
phase closes the collection scan, not necessarily its pending imports.

Authorization launch is not connection success. Accepted/cancelled/failed results
are request-correlated and saved without credentials. Confirmed reconnection of a
fenced source returns to review, not automatic permission. Unknown creation uses
same-request inspection. Internal navigation says Back to Calendar; Android
settings labels refer only to actual OS destinations.

Sync linked reminders separates Ready to turn on from Needs review. Independent
selection uses checkboxes; blocked rows have a reason and contextual review action
without a disabled radio. The title, source, explanation and action form one
padded unit. Returning from individual review preserves existing selections and
does not silently select newly eligible work. Resume/recovered-alert reviews use
the same anatomy. Incoming candidates say No Remilo alert yet.

Use the existing opaque atmosphere surfaces, neutral information glyphs and
semantic warning icon/text. Keep 16 dp insets, connected corners/seams, clipped
press/focus feedback, 48 dp secondary and 56 dp primary/review targets. Apple
hierarchy/feedback principles do not authorize iOS-specific control styling.

## Publication recovery feedback — 16 October 2026

Check publication result shows a busy indicator until the bounded native check
returns, followed by its confirmed, cancelled or still-unconfirmed result even
when the saved state did not change. Read-only checks never restore reminders or
reactivate a retired association; do not claim a removed local alert is still
kept, or that a separately restored reminder is inactive. Calendar preference
reasons use localized explanations, never internal outcome codes. The Agenda
banner uses one localized plural message for its Review count so words cannot
run together. Retired automatic publications do not remain actionable review
items; their sent identities stay available for read-only inspection.

## Independent copies and cancellation

Google deletion never stops Remilo delivery. Once observed, active Agenda rows
show Google event deleted with mode-specific local consequences and spoken text.
Details shows the actual scheduled/postponed/intended/ringing/elapsed presentation,
not a promise of audibility, plus Move reminder to Trash and Calendar details.
Completed/skipped/trashed reminders retain the remote condition inside read-only
context. Offline failures preserve the last known deletion. Missing/inaccessible
responses say unavailable rather than deleted. Fresh remote availability clears
the cancellation notice but requires confirmed review before linked updates resume.

Unlink keeps both copies, local scheduling and existing Google reminder settings.
Its confirmation identifies those effects and conditionally explains that a sent
request may still finish. Relinking needs fresh identity/ownership/field review.
Local Trash cancels Remilo delivery only; shared Trash/Purge confirmation states
Google Calendar will not be changed. Restore retains work state and never replays
past alerts or previously approved Google writes. No automatic deletion/restoration
or Google reminder restoration is introduced.

Individual and all-event import explain that the reminder has its own alert and
that deleting Google does not stop it. Default Alarm, future-time validation,
explicit Google reminder consent and per-side outcome reporting are unchanged.

## Calendar attention and Sync details — 14 October 2026

Calendar management orders the 24sp semibold title, actual local alert context
(18sp semibold value), current synchronization status/recovery, compact source,
collapsed Sync details and lower neutral Unlink group. Body/supporting text is
16/14sp and scales/wraps. Actual timing uses the shared native-derived local
presentation, including Snooze/Postpone, No alert, intended Blocked/Pending and
terminal timing. Google event time never substitutes for a local alert.

Healthy automatic permission is neutral **Automatic sync on**, with at most one
Last synced line. Successful explicit checks use a green check plus truthful
wording. Current review/uncertainty is amber; current access/blocking failures are
red. Pending, intentional Pause and Local only remain neutral. Color supplements
text/icons, and historical failure timestamps do not make a current error.

Sync details contains source/zone, agreement/observation/Google-update/check
timestamps, field/local-only explanation, optional per-reminder Sync now or
read-only Check for changes, and **Recent Calendar changes**. It retains its open
state during refresh and stays open during an interaction within it. Current
Review changes, Reconnect Google and Check update result remain outside it.
Recent Calendar changes filters the existing Activity projection by Calendar
entries, retains family/origin context and shows recorded field groups without
inventing values. General Activity entrypoints remain unchanged.

Agenda displays a single surfaced, inset 16dp **Calendar items need review** action
with icon, consequence, global count, Review N items and a chevron. It includes
reminders, incoming candidates and setup issues, deduplicates families and uses
native severity across the full issue set. Hide it at zero; preserve local alarm
problems/ringing priorities and Agenda filters. Ordinary reminder cards are unchanged.

The overview keeps global Sync now secondary. Its explicit progress/result stays
inside the leading status card; unresolved current issues outrank old success.
Connected Calendar disclosures, including Sync controls and Sync details, use
the surrounding settings rows' 16dp inset, 40dp neutral icon container and label
alignment. Keep a supplied summary visible in both states so expanding or
collapsing changes only the chevron and revealed content, without moving the
header icon. Expanded content stays padded inside its parent surface; omit the
standalone disclosure's bottom rule and clip press/focus feedback to the item.
Pending/held counts appear only when nonzero. Required sync permission is static
**Automatic sync included/on** information; an editable switch appears only when
the choice is supported. Ordinary unimported candidates retain **No Remilo alert
yet**. Identity/adoption issues direct existing-copy review without claiming an
already existing local reminder has no alert.

Explicit actions show progress and confirmed, scoped outcomes. Capture is not
completion, agreement is not a content update, and incomplete inspection cannot
say No changes found. A lost acknowledgement retains the same request identity.
Routine background success stays quiet. Inline feedback is preferred; an action
that removes its context may use the existing accessible snackbar. External link
failure has an inline explanation. Secondary targets remain 48dp and review/
primary actions 56dp, with clipped press/focus response and wrapping labels.

## Calendar Settings status and controls — 29 October 2026

The approved four-state mock guides Settings composition: Up to date, Syncing,
Paused and Error. It does not replace native recovery or permission semantics.
This correction supersedes the overview's action-first Sync controls disclosure
and its use of Automatic sync on as the only healthy status. Per-reminder timing,
relationship presentation and the existing setup/review flows remain governed by
their current contracts.

The leading card reports the latest native-confirmed synchronization state,
separately from whether automatic transfers are enabled. A green Up to date state
requires the latest completed, current-scope AllAuthorized request and native
freshness checks across the entire authorized scope. Show its completed-check
timestamp as supporting text. A per-link comparison, managed observation or old
successful timestamp cannot establish a complete global check. Navigation may
restore the saved result without dispatching a new check; it must not turn an
unknown or incomplete result into success.

Only native Checking work is presented as syncing. Queued/offline continuation
remains waiting, with existing retry/recovery behavior. Current access failures,
review issues, pending/held work, uncertain outcomes and changed scope override
old success. Preserve contextual recovery rather than flattening every problem
into Reconnect: access needs reconnection, conflicts need review and uncertain
writes need outcome inspection. Paused stays neutral and explains that local
reminders and alerts continue. Resume opens the existing reviewed native flow;
the mock's simplified transition grants no new permission.

Replace Sync controls with a state-first **Automatic sync** disclosure. Its saved
**On / Paused / Off** value and supporting explanation remain visible when open
or closed. The value describes configuration, not proof of freshness. Enabled but
blocked sync keeps its configuration visible and explains the current blocker.
Use existing connected-row geometry, neutral icon container, typography, pressed
and focus treatment; let enlarged labels and values wrap/stack without clipping.
The disclosure reports managed policy only: another authorized calendar's global
progress or error must not change its permission explanation. Valid global work
and recovery remain visible even while the selected calendar is Paused or Off.
Short scope labels sit beside the status and managed control; the full scope
explanation belongs in Sync details. Omit a zero-count support row entirely so
it cannot reserve a seam or take the last visible row's outside corners.
Pause and Turn off remain lower-emphasis contextual actions inside the disclosure.
The leading card retains a prominent Resume, Review or Reconnect when required;
healthy global Sync now remains secondary. Turning off retains the existing
themed confirmation and independent-data consequences.

Global Sync now retains **AllAuthorized** scope. Therefore Settings status and
completed-check wording cover all authorized synchronization, not just the named
managed calendar shown in the source row. State this scope honestly; individual
reminders can remain local only. Pause/Turn off and managed preferences apply to
the managed calendar; other calendars retain their own link permissions. The
mock's narrower calendar-only status explanation is superseded by this existing
native scope. No automatic new authorization or manual-link activation is added.

Use canonical success/error roles with explicit text and icons, neutral waiting
and Pause, and warnings for review/uncertainty. Preserve all four atmospheres,
independent brightness, English/Simplified Chinese, readable large text, 48 dp
secondary and 56 dp primary targets, and accessible state/action distinctions.

## Appearance, accessibility and rationale

Use the approved eight atmosphere/brightness pairs, connected geometry, at least
48 dp targets, wrapping 56 dp primary review actions and current device clock
formatting. Preserve long English/Chinese, 200% text, landscape, reduced motion,
keyboard and Back behavior. Routine Calendar administration stays secondary;
an actionable exception is concise and follows primary local timing.

do not experimentally prescribe this exact cross-app behavior.
