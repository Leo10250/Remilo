## Source checks and assessment

All four files decode as four-channel PNGs with actual alpha. Transparent pixel fractions:
M1 58.48%, M2 61.07%, S1 53.86%, S2 62.00%. Remaining pixels use varying alpha;
no alpha normalization or repainting was applied. The Meadow central lower third
(x 0.16–0.84, y 2/3–1) has negligible alpha; Sunrise upper halves are essentially clear
(S1 mean alpha 0.0061, S2 rounds to 0). This checks source transparency, not text contrast.

The [assessment](../design/artwork/p01-artwork-r1/README.md) covers artistic similarity,
composition, environmental coverage, crop anchors and future integration limits for each
source. Recommendation for owner consideration is M1 + S1: balanced canopy and a quieter
Agenda foreground. M2 offers a stronger right-hand sweep; S2 connects botanical corners
to the Butter reference. Meadow leaves are larger/more veined and S2 foreground heavier
than the originals. These differences are visible, not claimed resolved by color similarity.

No source is a final phone canvas. Sun/horizon retention, side-leaf clearance, visible terrain
coverage, 200% text, dark treatments and expanded layouts need actual-component inspection
after selected-art approval. No source-only alpha measurement establishes accessibility.
